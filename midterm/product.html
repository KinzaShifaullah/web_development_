Noir Ember

Rs. 3,490

Product Description

Noir Ember is a captivating fragrance inspired by confidence, sophistication and timeless elegance. Its character brings together smoky woods and warm amber, creating a deep and memorable scent for those who appreciate a refined presence.

The opening introduces a warm, expressive impression before settling into a rich woody character. As the fragrance develops, amber adds softness and depth, while smoky notes give it a distinctive finish. The overall profile is designed to feel elegant without being overpowering.

Wear Noir Ember for evening occasions, special gatherings or whenever you want a confident signature scent. Apply to pulse points and allow the fragrance to settle naturally.

Fragrance character: warm, woody, smoky and amber-inspired.
